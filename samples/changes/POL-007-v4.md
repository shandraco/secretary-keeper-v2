# Lockout/Tagout

## 1 Purpose
This policy sets the minimum rules for isolating machines from energy before servicing.

## 2 Definitions
2.1 "Isolation point" means a valve, breaker, switch or blind flange that stops energy, including stored energy, from reaching a machine.
2.2 "Authorized worker" means a worker trained in this policy within the last 12 months.

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 Supervisors keep the list of authorized workers current.

## 4 Lockout devices
4.1 Each authorized worker is issued a personal lock and tag.
4.2 Lockout devices are inspected before every shift by the authorized worker using them.
4.3 Damaged devices are removed from service immediately.

## 5 Procedure
5.1 Notify affected workers before applying locks.
5.2 Apply locks at every isolation point and verify zero energy before starting work.
5.3 Stored energy must be released or restrained before zero energy is verified.
