# Confined Space Entry

## 3 Before entry
3.1 Before entry, all energy sources are isolated under POL-007.
3.2 The entry attendant stays outside the space at all times.

## 4 Air testing
4.1 Air is tested for oxygen and flammable gas before entry and every hour.
