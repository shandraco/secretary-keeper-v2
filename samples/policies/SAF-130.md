# Contractor Safety

## 1 Scope
1.1 Applies to every contractor working on site.

## 2 Energy control
2.1 Contractor staff count as authorized workers only if trained within the last 24 months.
2.2 Contractors apply their own locks at each isolation point and hand over keys at shift end.

## 3 Induction
3.1 All contractors complete the site induction on their first day.
