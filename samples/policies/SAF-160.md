# Forklift Operation

## 1 Licences
1.1 Only licensed drivers operate forklifts.

## 2 Daily checks
2.1 Drivers complete the pre-use checklist at the start of each shift.
2.2 Faulty trucks are tagged out of use and reported to the workshop.
