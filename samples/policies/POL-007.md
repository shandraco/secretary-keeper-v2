# Lockout/Tagout

## 1 Purpose
This policy sets the minimum rules for isolating machines from energy before servicing.

## 2 Definitions
2.1 "Isolation point" means a valve, breaker or switch that physically stops energy reaching a machine.
2.2 "Authorized worker" means a worker trained in this policy within the last 24 months.

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 Supervisors keep the list of authorized workers current.

## 4 Lockout devices
4.1 Each authorized worker is issued a personal lock and tag.
4.2 Lockout devices are inspected weekly by the area supervisor.
4.3 Damaged devices are removed from service immediately.

## 5 Procedure
5.1 Notify affected workers before applying locks.
5.2 Apply locks at every isolation point and verify zero energy before starting work.
