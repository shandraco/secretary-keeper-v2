# Personal Protective Equipment

## 1 Requirements
1.1 Safety glasses and hearing protection are worn on the production floor.
1.2 Cut-resistant gloves are worn when handling sheet metal.

## 2 Issue and replacement
2.1 Stores issue PPE free of charge and replace damaged items on request.
