# Maintenance Access

## 1 Scope
1.1 Applies to all planned and breakdown maintenance on production lines.

## 2 Before work starts
2.1 Maintenance staff follow the lockout procedure in POL-007 §5 before opening any guard.
2.2 Lockout devices used on the line are checked weekly, as required by POL-007 §4.2.

## 3 Records
3.1 The shift lead logs every lockout in the maintenance system.
